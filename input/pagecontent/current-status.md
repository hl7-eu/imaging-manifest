{{iheRad}} has finished the international version of the {{iheMado}}. The international version is missing some elements that required in the European scope. This EU version of MADO includes those elements. As {{iheRad}} is limited to FHIR-R4, the EU imaging manifest specification will be limited to FHIR-R4 as well. At a later point in time, an R5 version might be added.

The workgroup [HL7 EU Imaging Group](https://confluence.hl7.org/spaces/HEU/pages/248712142/Imaging+Study+Report+Project+Edition+1) is currently updating the EU specific MADO specification. When finished it will be balloted through HL7eu and IHEeu under the flag of EURIDICE.

 The current builds of the implementation guide can be found at the following URLs:

* [HL7 Europe Imaging Manifest R4](https://build.fhir.org/ig/hl7-eu/imaging-manifest/en/)
